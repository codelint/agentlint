Privacy Policy for Codelint

Effective date: 2026-10-20


1. Overview

Codelint is an AI coding assistant client for iPhone and iPad. It is designed as a tool:
you provide the model endpoint, the workspace files and the target hosts, and the app
performs operations only within the scope you specify.

Codelint can be used in two ways:

1. Bring your own model: you enter your own large-model API endpoint and key, and your
   conversations go directly from your device to that endpoint. We are not involved.
2. Built-in models: after signing in, you may use the built-in model quota we provide. In
   that case your conversation content is relayed through our server to the model provider.

To provide accounts, subscription quota and built-in models, Codelint operates a
developer-run server (https://ios.codelint.com). Except for the items listed below, we do
not collect, store or upload your files, source code or conversation content.


2. Information we collect

2.1 Account information (collected when you sign in)

Signing in is optional: the full feature set of bring-your-own-model works without an
account.

- Sign in with Apple: we receive the user identifier (sub) provided by Apple, plus your
  email (which may be Apple's private relay address) and name if you choose to share them.
- Email code sign-in: we receive the email address you enter and send a one-time code to
  that address.

2.2 Sign-in credentials and devices

- After sign-in, the server issues an access credential (secret) that is stored only in
  your device's secure storage (Keychain).
- To display and manage sign-in devices, the server records the client-generated device
  identifier (duid), the most recent sign-in time and the source IP address.

2.3 Subscriptions and purchases

Subscriptions are purchased through Apple App Store in-app purchase. We never receive your
payment method, card number or billing address. We only receive the transaction from Apple
to verify the subscription tier and expiry date, and we store your tier and quota on our
server.

2.4 Built-in model calls (when signed in and using built-in quota)

When you use a built-in model, conversation content, tool-call arguments and tool results
are relayed through our server to the model provider. The server only records usage
information needed for quota accounting: model name, upstream domain, request ID, request
size in bytes, token usage, response status and latency.

The server does not store conversation content, source code or tool result content.

2.5 Feedback (only when you submit it)

When you submit feedback in the app, we receive the title, the body, any contact details
you choose to provide, and client environment information (app version and build, OS
version, device model, language and region, time zone, free storage, current persona
name). Environment information is used to reproduce problems and never includes your
workspace or conversation content.

2.6 Usage and quota

The server records, per account, the number of built-in model calls and token usage, to
settle and display your quota.

2.7 What we do not do

- No advertising identifier (IDFA) and no cross-app tracking
- No third-party advertising SDKs and no third-party analytics SDKs
- No access to contacts, call history or messages
- No background location tracking
- We never sell or rent your personal information


3. Services you configure yourself

The following data goes where you decide; we neither receive nor retain it:

1. Model service
   The large-model API endpoint and key you enter in Settings. When you start a
   conversation or invoke a tool, the conversation content, code snippets and tool output
   are sent to the endpoint you specified. How that data is handled depends on that
   provider's policy; please refer to the privacy policy of the provider you choose.
   To help the model provider diagnose calls, request headers carry a User-Agent with the
   app name and version, OS version, device model and system locale. They never include
   your account, IP address, device unique identifier or conversation content.

2. SSH hosts
   The host address, port, user name and key that you add are used only to connect to your
   own hosts, and are stored on your device.

3. MCP services
   Requests are sent only to the MCP endpoints that you configure (these may be local
   network or remote addresses).

4. Optional self-hosted proxy component
   The project source includes an optional proxy component that you may deploy yourself to
   forward and measure model calls. If you deploy it and point your model requests at it,
   the traffic passes through your own server. We do not provide a hosted version of this
   component.


4. Data stored on your device

The following data is stored locally on your device (SwiftData and the app sandbox):

- Workspace files and directories, and file edit history
- Sessions, conversations, skills and prompts
- Model endpoints and keys, SSH host configuration, MCP service configuration
- Sign-in credential (Keychain only)
- App runtime logs and event records (used only for local troubleshooting)

You can remove any of these by deleting the corresponding item in the app; uninstalling
the app removes all local data.


5. Device permissions and how they are used

Codelint requests system permissions only for the purposes below, and each one can be
denied in system settings:

- Local network: to connect to MCP services on the same local network
- Photo library (add only): to save images from the workspace to your photo library
- Media library: to search for and play songs from your Music app
- Microphone and speech recognition: to turn your speech into text in the input box
- Location (when in use): your approximate location is obtained only when you explicitly
  ask a question that needs location context (for example "the network environment near
  me", "my current location" or "cafes nearby"), to be used as conversation context sent
  to the AI, and to display the Wi-Fi network you are connected to. Your location is never
  tracked in the background.
- Notifications: to alert you locally about task results (no server-side push)
- Clipboard: written only when you actively tap a Copy button in the interface. The app
  does not provide any AI tool that reads the clipboard automatically, and never reads
  clipboard contents without your confirmation.

Denying a permission only disables the corresponding feature; all other features continue
to work.


6. Data sharing and third parties

We share data with third parties only when necessary:

- Apple: Sign in with Apple (identity verification) and in-app purchase (subscription
  verification), subject to Apple's privacy policy
- Model providers: only when you use a built-in model, our server relays your request
- Email provider: to send sign-in verification codes and feedback receipts

Other than the cases above and where required by law, we do not provide your data to third
parties.


7. Retention and deletion

- On-device data: you may delete sessions, files or configuration inside the app at any
  time; uninstalling the app deletes all local data.
- Account data (email, Apple user identifier, subscription tier, quota and usage, sign-in
  device records): retained while your account exists. You may request account deletion by
  emailing codelint@foxmail.com; we will delete or anonymize the data within 15 business
  days, except where retention is required by law.
- The server does not store conversation content, so there is no conversation copy to
  delete separately.


8. Security

- Communication between the app and our server uses HTTPS (TLS) encryption.
- Sign-in credentials are stored in the system Keychain; model keys and SSH keys are stored
  in the Keychain or the local sandbox according to your choice.
- Server-side account data is stored on a least-privilege basis and access is restricted.


9. Children's privacy

Codelint is intended for developers and is not directed to children under 16. We do not
knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided
personal information to us, please contact us and we will delete it.


10. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will note them in the release notes of a new
version and update the effective date at the top. Continued use means you accept the
updated policy.


11. Contact us

For questions about this policy, or to access or delete your personal information, please
contact: codelint@foxmail.com
